# edarra — Schedule F on the accrual method, a nursery with a loss

A made-up taxpayer. Every name and amount is invented. Synthetic and
**unverified**: no answer key stands behind the expected values. They are the
engine's output, checked by hand line by line (below), then locked.

## Who

Edarra Vashenko, single, a full-year California resident. Her salary is
$420,000. She started a grapevine nursery in 2025 on the accrual method, with
inventory at cost, leased land and equipment (no depreciation), and material
participation. Part of the start-up was a nonrecourse loan from the seller of
the stock, so some investment is not at risk (box 36b).

## What it covers

- Schedule F Part III with every line filled (37 to 50), and Part I line 9.
- A loss that Form 6198 limits to the amount at risk ($340,000 of $388,000).
- Form 461 line 6, and an excess business loss of $27,000 on Schedule 1 line 8p.
- Schedule CA (540) line 6 column A and FTB 3461 line 6.
- The farm's QBI loss carried forward (Form 8995-A Schedule C).

This is the second scenario for Schedule F: a loss, the accrual method and a
California return exclude a profit on the cash method with Schedule SE (daise).
