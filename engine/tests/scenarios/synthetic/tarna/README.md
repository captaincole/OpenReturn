# tarna (synthetic, unverified)

Tarna Wexley is a made-up taxpayer. No filed return, benchmark case or answer
key stands behind her values. The lines below were checked by hand against
the forms and their instructions, then the engine's output was locked.

## Who she is

- Single, a full-year California resident (Vallejo).
- She repairs marine engines alone (Schedule C): $160,000 of receipts,
  $90,000 of profit. Her shop is at Mare Island, a former LAMBRA; her yard in
  Vallejo is outside it.
- Three fifths of the business property is in the former LAMBRA. She has no
  employees, so Worksheet I uses the property factor alone.
- Her business lost money in 2011 and 2012, and she elected the LAMBRA NOL.
  Her 2024 FTB 3807 carried $20,000 (2011) and $50,000 (2012) to 2025.
- She never had LAMBRA credits.

## Documents

None. The worksheets of the FTB 3807 booklet stay with her records, and the
amounts come from her books, her asset ledger and her 2024 return.

## What it covers

- FTB 3807 items A to H, line 2 (one factor) and Part III (Worksheet II).
- The deduction is limited to the LAMBRA business income: the 2011 loss is
  used in full, the 2012 loss in part, and $16,000 carries to 2026.
- Her modified AGI is under $1,000,000, so the deduction is not suspended.
- Schedule CA (540) line 9b3 takes the deduction, and Form 540 line 14 with it.
- Schedule Z and lines 1a to 1d stay blank: she has no credit. The sister
  scenario `shemerin` covers the credits.
