# marin — a citrus grower with empowerment zone hands (synthetic)

Marin Velasquez is single and lives in McAllen, Texas. Every name and amount here is made up. There is no outside source and no answer key: the expected values are the engine's output, checked by hand against the form and its instructions, then locked (`verification: "unverified"`).

Marin owns a citrus grove (Schedule F) in the Rio Grande Valley empowerment zone. Three hands worked on it in 2025. One of them is from a targeted group, so $6,000 of his wages also figure a work opportunity credit (Form 5884, stated). She takes the standard deduction. The return is federal only: Texas has no income tax.

The scenario is the farm branch of Form 8844, which `jaret` (a business) cannot show: the engine figures the QBI of one self-employed activity at a time.

- Line 1: Eliseo's $15,000 limit is reduced by his $6,000 of work opportunity credit wages. Hector lives outside the zone and does not count.
- Line 2: 20% of line 1. Schedule F line 22 takes labor hired less both credits.
- Line 3 is blank: no pass-through credit. Line 4 goes to Form 3800 Part III line 3; the work opportunity credit goes to line 4b.

Forms that file: Form 1040, Schedules 1, 2, 3, F and SE, Forms 3800, 6251, 8844 and 8995.
