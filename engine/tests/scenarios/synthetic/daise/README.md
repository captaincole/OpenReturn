# daise — Schedule F on the cash method, a ranch with a profit

A made-up taxpayer. Every name and amount is invented. Synthetic and
**unverified**: no answer key stands behind the expected values. They are the
engine's output, checked by hand line by line (below), then locked.

## Who

Daise Thornbury, 67, single, a full-year Texas resident (no state return). She
runs a cattle and hay ranch on the cash method and materially participates.
She also has bank interest of $85,000 and social security benefits of $34,800.
She paid $66,000 of estimated tax.

## What it covers

- Schedule F Parts I and II with every income line filled: purchased cattle
  resold (1a to 1c), cooperative distributions with a part not taxable (3a,
  3b), agricultural program payments with a market gain under the CCC loan
  election and excluded cost-sharing payments (4a, 4b), CCC loans under the
  election and a forfeit above the basis (5a to 5c), crop insurance deferred to
  2026 and brought in from 2024 (6a to 6d), custom hire and a fuel tax refund.
- Every expense line: the standard mileage rate (line 10), conservation
  expenses under the 25% limit (line 12), five other expenses and the section
  263A capitalization in parentheses on line 32f.
- The farm's line 34 on Schedule 1 line 6, Schedule SE line 1a, and $12,000 of
  CRP payments while on social security on Schedule SE line 1b.
- The farm as her QBI business (Form 8995-A, above the threshold) and the
  nonpassive farm taken out of net investment income on Form 8960 line 4b.
  Her Forms 1099-PATR are from a farm supply cooperative with no qualified
  payments (box 7), so Form 8995-A line 14 has no patron reduction.

## Documents
