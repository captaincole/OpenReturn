# Form 8820 — open points

Form 8820 (Rev. September 2018) is the current form for 2025; its instructions are in the same PDF (`irs-8820-form-2025`). The instructions publish no example with numbers, and a web search found none. Every rule below is a stated rule or the author's reading.

## Two texts conflict

Each item has a `DECISION` comment and a row in `docs/rule-decisions.md`.

1. **The deduction goes down by line 2a, not line 2c** (A119). The instructions: "reduce your otherwise allowable deduction ... by the amount of the credit on this line" (`irs-8820-form-2025::sec::line-2a::b02`, line 2a). The engine follows them (`lines.ts`). Dollars change only when line 2b holds an amount: with line 2a $50,000 and line 2b $900, the engine reduces the deduction by $50,000, and the statute's reading by $49,100. No scenario has both (jon has line 2b but elects the reduced credit). **For `docs/law-findings.md`:** section 280C(b)(1) disallows the part "equal to the amount of the credit allowable for the taxable year under section 45C" (`usc-26-2025::sec::s280c::b-1`), and section 45P(c) reduces the credit otherwise allowable by the Form 8932 credit on the same wages (`usc-26-2025::sec::s45p::c`). Read together, the statute points to line 2c.
2. **Which Schedule C lines give up the reduction** (A120, `reduction.ts`). The instructions leave the lines to the taxpayer ("Identify the lines of your return ... on which the reductions were made", `line-2a::b02`). The engine takes it from line 26 (wages), line 22 (supplies), line 20a (computer rental) and line 11 (contract testing), in that order, each line by at most the clinical expenses of its kind. This is the order the research credit's Section F uses (`federal/6765/reduction.ts`, row A114).
3. **Line 3 rounds each K-1 before the add** (A121), the rule of Form 8846 line 5 (A96), so line 4 equals Form 3800 line 1h. The blind fill (2026-11-27) took the Form 1040 rule instead: K-1s of $3,000.40 and $1,200.40 give $4,201 there and $4,200 in the engine. The engine keeps one rule for every credit form that sends K-1 credits to Form 3800 (unit test in `tests/unit/federal/8820/form.test.ts`).

## Should we do this now

4. **Capitalized expenses.** Not now: "In a case in which qualified clinical testing expenses are capitalized ... reduce the amount chargeable to capital account" (`line-2a::b02`; section 280C(b)(2)). The fact holds only deducted Schedule C expenses, and no scenario capitalizes them. A reduction the lines cannot take is a gap; the engine never reduces a capital account.
5. **Two credits on the same wages.** Done: Form 8820 is one entry of the list of credits that lower a deduction (`facts/kinds/activitiesOnReturn.ts`), after the work opportunity, empowerment zone, Form 8932 and 8994, tip, disabled access, retirement plan and childcare credits. Its reduction is placed on each line as those credits left it, so a reduction the lines cannot take is a gap, never a dollar that goes unused.
6. **Controlled group or common control.** Not needed: "Your credit is determined on a proportionate basis to your share of the aggregate clinical testing expenses" (`member-of-controlled-group::b01`). The credit is a flat 25%, so the share gives the same dollars as the member's own expenses. The attached statement and "See Attached" are not printed.
7. **The attached statements.** Not now: line 2a asks for a statement with "No" (the reduced deduction lines) and for a group member. The engine prints no statements for any form yet.
8. **One fact per return, one business.** Not now: the fact names the one Schedule C business that deducts the expenses. A taxpayer with two testing businesses cannot state both; no scenario has two.
9. **Ineligible testing and funded amounts.** Not needed in the engine: testing outside the United States (`testing-not-eligible-for-the::b01`) and amounts funded by a grant or another person (`purpose-of-form::b01`) are left out by the taxpayer, because the fact holds qualified amounts only.
10. **Line 2b is asked only for the return's own Form 8932 credit.** Done: a stated Form 3800 line 1w credit with no pass-through EIN, when line 1 holds wages. A K-1 differential wage credit is the entity's wages, not the taxpayer's. A stated line 2b with no such credit, or more than it, is a gap.
11. **Lines 5 and 6** are for estates and trusts. Done: an individual stops at line 4, so they stay blank.
12. **Part II holds 26 drugs.** Done: "Attach as many Part II pages as needed" (`part-ii::b01`). Each further 26 drugs print on another copy's page 2 (`copies.ts`, the copies entry in `federal/index.ts`); page 1 prints once.
13. **The decision `decisions.scope.must_file_8820` = false with a claimed credit.** Done: the Schedule C reduction reads the fact, not the decision, so Form 3800 line 1h is a gap until the two agree (as Form 8846 does). With the election, nothing is reduced and there is no gap.
14. **A passive Schedule C business** (line G "No"). Not now: the credit is a passive activity credit, and the engine does not figure a passive Schedule C business. Form 3800 line 1h is a gap; Form 8820's own lines still compute.
15. **Computer rental** (from the blind fill). Not in the engine: the Form 8820 instructions count "amounts ... that would be described as qualified research expenses under section 41" (`irs-8820-form-2025::sec::purpose-of-form::b01`). Form 6765 adds conditions for computer rental: "The computers must be located off your premises, and you must not be the operator or primary user of the computers" (`irs-6765-inst-2025::sec::section-g-columns-50-56::b03`). The fact holds qualified amounts only, so the taxpayer (and Cade) apply the conditions. A rental that fails them changes line 1 by its full amount; Cade's skill should ask about the conditions.
16. **Contract testing paid to a university** (from the blind fill). Settled by the text: Form 6765 takes 100% only for energy research by a university and 65% for other contract research (`irs-6765-inst-2025::sec::section-g-columns-50-56::b04`). Form 8820 says "100% (instead of 65% or 75%) of contract research expenses are treated as clinical testing expenses" (`purpose-of-form::b01`), with no exception for the payee. The engine and the blind fill both take 100%.
17. **California.** Done: FTB Pub. 1001: "Enter the amount of the federal Orphan Drug Credit that reduced the federal deduction for qualified clinical testing expenses on Schedule CA (540), Part I ... line 3 ... column B" (`ftb-pub-1001-2025::sec::enter-on-schedule-ca-540-part-i-or-7::b08`). The engine adds the reduction on every Schedule C line it lowered to the column B amount of the credits California gives back (`businessCreditAddBack`; the entry's `californiaAddsBack`). Schedule CA (540NR) runs the same rule. California's own orphan drug credit is repealed; its carryover (FTB 3801-CR code 185, `ca-3801cr-inst-2025::sec::c-who-must-file::b01`) is a separate fact that this form does not touch.
18. **The Form 8932 credit lowers Schedule C wages** (from the tax review). Done: "Enter the total salaries and wages for the tax year reduced by the amount of the following credit(s) ... Credit for Employer Differential Wage Payments (Form 8932). Employer Credit for Paid Family and Medical Leave (Form 8994)" (`irs-1040sc-inst-2025::sec::line-26::b01`). Before, a stated Form 8932 or 8994 credit went to Form 3800 and Schedule C kept the whole wages. Now the stated credit of the taxpayer's own employees names its business (`businessKey`), and `statedWageCreditsByBusiness` (`facts/kinds/generalBusinessCredit.ts`) is one entry of `BUSINESS_CREDIT_REDUCTIONS`. A credit that names no Schedule C business is a gap on its Form 3800 line (`3800/sources.ts`). A credit a pass-through entity allocated lowers nothing, as line 26 says. jon's line 26 is $187,600 ($190,000 less $2,400).
19. **California gives the Form 8932 and 8994 wages back.** Done, by the rule the engine already uses for Form 8844 (8844 QUESTIONS.md item 11): the FTB names no adjustment for either credit, but "The provisions of Section 280C(a) of the Internal Revenue Code (relating to rule for employment credits) shall not apply" (`ca-rtc-2025::sec::s17270::b`), and section 280C(a) is the rule for "sections 45A(a), 45P(a), 45S(a)(1)(A), 51(a), and 1396(a)" (`usc-26-2025::sec::s280c::a`), which covers both credits. `businessCreditAddBack` includes them in Schedule CA line 3 column B with no code of its own. No scenario has a California Form 8932 credit (unit test in `tests/unit/statedWageCredits.test.ts`).
20. **The Form 8994 premiums credit.** Not now: section 280C(a) also takes "that portion of the paid family and medical leave credit which is determined ... under section 45S(a)(1)(B)" off the premiums, not the wages (`usc-26-2025::sec::s280c::a`). The 2025 Schedule C instructions list Form 8994 under wages only (`line-26::b01`), and the engine takes the whole stated credit off line 26. A premiums credit would belong on line 15 (insurance); no scenario has one.
21. **Schedule F labor hired.** Not now: Schedule F line 22 reduces labor hired by the employment credits too (`irs-1040sf-inst-2025::sec::line-22::b01`). `businessKey` names a Schedule C business only, so a farm's Form 8932 credit is a gap on Form 3800 line 1w.
22. **jon's K-1 has no box 14 code A or box 20 code Z.** Not now: a real general partner's K-1 would show the self-employment earnings and the section 199A information, which would change Schedule SE and Form 8995-A. Form 8820 reads neither, and the scenario's values follow its facts as stated.

## Pattern change

23. **A stated general business credit can name its business.** Before: a credit stated on Form 3800 (`general_business_credit.current.*`) named no activity, so nothing on Schedule C could follow it. After: a stated Form 8932 or 8994 credit of the taxpayer's own employees carries `businessKey`, and Schedule C line 26 goes down by it. Cade has no record tool for stated credits yet; when it gets one, the tool asks for the business.

## Form 8820 next to Form 6765

- **The same expenses cannot count twice.** "Qualified clinical testing expenses used to figure the orphan drug credit cannot also be used to figure the credit for increasing research activities" (`irs-8820-form-2025::sec::coordination-with-the::b01`; section 45C(c)(1), `usc-26-2025::sec::s45c::c`). Both facts state totals, so the engine cannot see which dollars each one counts. It sees one case: on the business both credits name, a Schedule C line that holds one kind of both (line 26 with clinical and research wages, line 22, line 20a, line 11) is less than the two amounts together. Then some dollars are in both, or one of them is not deducted on that line, and the facts cannot tell which. Form 6765 line 28 is then a gap, and Schedule C takes no research reduction (`coordination.ts`). When each line holds both amounts, the engine takes the facts as stated: each holds qualified amounts only (item 9), and Cade's skill must ask for research expenses without the clinical testing expenses.
- **Both reductions on the same Schedule C lines.** Done. Form 8820 comes before Form 6765 in the list of credits that lower a deduction, and both take lines 26, 22, 20a and 11 in that order. Form 6765 places line 28 on what Form 8820 left, each line by at most what is left and what the business paid in that category, so no dollar leaves a line twice. California gives the orphan drug reduction back (item 17); the research reduction stays Form 6765's own Schedule CA line 3 gap. A unit test holds one business with both (`tests/unit/federal/creditsThatLowerDeductions.test.ts`).
- **Base period.** "Any of these expenses that are also qualified research expenses must be included in base period research expenses when figuring the credit ... in a later tax year" (`coordination-with-the::b01`; section 45C(c)(2)). Form 6765's prior-year QREs (`irs-6765-inst-2025::sec::line-21::b01`) are stated answers; Cade must include the orphan drug expenses in them. No engine change.
- **Form 6765 line 27 takes off the Form 8932 credit on the same wages too** (`irs-6765-inst-2025::sec::section-c-current-year-credit::b01`), like Form 8820 line 2b. Not now: each form reads its own stated part. Both should read the same own Form 8932 credit (`ownDifferentialWageCredit` in `figures.ts`).

## Proposals for a discussion

- **One placement module for both research credits.** `federal/8820/reduction.ts` and `federal/6765/reduction.ts` have the same order and the same rule, and each places on what the credits before it left. One shared function could place both. This changes how two forms reduce Schedule C, so a person decides.

## Blind fill (2026-11-27)

A blind preparer filled 14 cases from the form and its instructions only. Every line matched the engine except case 9 (two K-1s with cents, no own testing): line 3 and line 4 were $4,201 against the engine's $4,200 (item 3 above), and line 2c was 0 against the engine's blank (blank against 0 only; "Figure any orphan drug credit from your own trade or business on lines 1 and 2a", `specific-instructions::b01`).
